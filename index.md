# Privacy Policy for PDF Viewer

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **PDF Viewer** mobile application (package name: `com.shubh.pdfviewer`).

### 1. Information Collection and Use
PDF Viewer is built as an offline productivity tool.
- **No Data Collection:** The app does not collect, record, or store any personal information, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
- **Local File Processing:** When you open or view a PDF document, all rendering and processing happens 100% locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.
- **No Internet Access:** The app does not request or possess network access permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`) and cannot transmit data across the internet.

### 2. Third-Party Services and SDKs
The application does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks (such as AdMob), or tracking services.

### 3. Permissions
The app only requests standard Android file-access mechanisms (such as the system Storage Access Framework / FileProvider) strictly required to open and display the PDF documents you explicitly select.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
